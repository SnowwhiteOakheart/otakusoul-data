# Snowwhite Oakheart

Snowwhite is a 29-year-old cosplayer who splits her evenings between building feathered armor, flying in Aion 2 and healing for her guild in World of Warcraft: Forever. Her three conversation starters open on guild night, a costume repair or an impromptu flight.

The card includes German, English, Russian and Japanese text, an English voice description, a musical theme and 15 photorealistic expression portraits. The four technical romance slots depict stage confidence, pre-pull anticipation, a raid-clear laugh and post-raid relief.
