## English

An independent, unofficial fan adventure after the Siege of Orgrimmar and before the Draenor expedition. A disused funeral bell in Tirisfal steals memories. Explore, negotiate, release its bonds or seal the artefact; several endings are possible.

The **World of Warcraft – Tirisfal** hub collection includes five original adult fan characters, 75 individually generated expressions (15 each), a 35-entry lorebook and a narrative stage for roughly 90 minutes. Profiles, lore entries and stage are available in German, English, Russian and Japanese. Cards have English voice descriptions. Refresh the hub and choose **Install all** in the collection to download cards, lorebook, stage and background. All five cards join the party; the narrator handles other NPCs. Player identity remains open. No 3D or Live2D model is required.
